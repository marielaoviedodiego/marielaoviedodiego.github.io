I study how interactions among organisms shape morphological, behavioral, and functional diversity. My work combines field ecology, behavioral experiments, comparative morphology, and 3D imaging.

## Species interactions and diversification

I investigate how competition and coexistence relate to ecological and morphological divergence in Malagasy pelican spiders.

## Functional morphology and prey capture

Using micro-CT and behavioral experiments, I examine how the structures involved in prey capture relate to performance.

## Sexual selection and reproductive interactions

My research on scorpions explores reproductive behavior, sexual selection, and interactions between species.

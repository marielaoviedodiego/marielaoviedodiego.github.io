Teaching and mentoring are important parts of my work as a scientist. I aim to help students connect biological questions with appropriate methods, interpret their results carefully, and communicate their ideas clearly.

## Teaching

I served as a Teaching Assistant and Adscript Professor in Entomology at the Universidad Nacional de Córdoba from 2018 to 2024. I also taught Biology at the Instituto de Enseñanza Domingo Faustino Sarmiento from 2024 to 2025.

My research experience allows me to support training in organismal biology, field methods, behavioral observation, morphological analysis, and quantitative approaches to biological data.

## Mentoring

I have supported students in developing research questions, collecting and analyzing data, interpreting results, and preparing scientific presentations and written work. I particularly enjoy helping early-career researchers build confidence in the practical steps of doing research.

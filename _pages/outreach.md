Arachnids offer a powerful way to introduce people to questions about biodiversity, behavior, and evolution. Their unfamiliar forms and remarkable natural histories can spark curiosity and create opportunities to discuss how scientists study the living world.

I value opportunities to share research beyond academic publications, communicate the importance of biodiversity, and make scientific methods more accessible to students and broader audiences.

This page will feature selected outreach activities, public presentations, and contributions to the arachnological community.

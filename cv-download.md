I am an evolutionary biologist and arachnologist currently working as a Peter Buck Postdoctoral Fellow at the Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History. My research integrates ecology, behavior, morphology, and imaging to investigate phenotypic diversification.

My academic training includes a PhD in Biological Sciences from the Universidad Nacional de Córdoba, Argentina. My research experience spans fieldwork, behavioral experiments, comparative approaches, museum collections, and quantitative analysis.

For publication details, visit my [ORCID profile](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-4469-6244). For questions or collaboration inquiries, contact me at [oviedodiegom@si.edu](mailto:oviedodiegom@si.edu).
